A theorem I find fascinating

The Newton–Leibniz formula (Fundamental Theorem of Calculus)

\[ \int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a), \quad \text{where } F'(x) = f(x) \]

This equation is known as the Newton–Leibniz theorem. I personally find it very interesting and beautiful, as it gives an elegant relationship between differentiation and integration.
