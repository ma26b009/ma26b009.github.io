My favorite equation

The derivative of an integral with moving limits

ddx ( ∫h(x)g(x) f(t)dt ) = f(g(x))·g′(x) − f(h(x))·h′(x)

One of the most beautiful equations I have learnt so far.

It follows from the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and the chain rule. It is a form of the Leibniz integral rule.

The shaded area is the integral of f(t) from h(x) to g(x). As x changes, both limits move. The page checks the formula against a numerical calculation, and they match.
